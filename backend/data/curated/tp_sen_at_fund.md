# SEN Fund for Assistive Technology (AT) — Specialised Support

The SEN funds you can tap on to apply for Assistive Technology (AT) depend on your specific needs. The fund covers students with physical impairment, learning, or behavioural conditions. These funds are set aside by the Ministry of Education to provide SEN-based, student-centric assistance to Polytechnic students with SEN. The funds will be used to purchase AT devices or support services for official course-related activities.

## Eligibility Criteria for the SEN Fund

To be eligible for SEN funding for Assistive Technology, a student must meet ALL of the following criteria:

- Singapore Citizen
- Full-time diploma student, or incoming student with a confirmed place in TP's enrolment
- Has declared their SEN condition to TP, with a statement of diagnosis from relevant medical professionals

Students who are eligible for and require SEN funding may contact the SEN Support Office for assistance.

## How to Apply for Assistive Technology

Eligible students can use the Ministry of Education's SEN fund to purchase Assistive Technology (AT) devices and/or support services. To find out more and start the process, make an appointment with the SEN Support Team at:

https://for.edu.sg/booktpsen

## What is Assistive Technology (AT)?

Assistive Technology refers to any item, equipment or product system that helps to maintain or improve an individual's functioning. TP has an AT library that allows students to loan and trial AT devices as part of the AT assessment process.
